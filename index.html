How It Works

Apply → Get Approved → Receive Grants

Three transparent steps from submission to disbursement. Funding ranges from $200,000 to $2,000,000 and is never repaid.

  1. 1

    Apply

    10 minutes

    Complete the four-phase applicant profile, choose your assessment tier, and submit. A tracking number is issued instantly.

  2. 2

    Get Approved

    Reviewed & notified

    The PBG Review Administration verifies your record and issues a determination to your chosen email or SMS channel.

  3. 3

    Receive Grants

    7–14 days

    Approved funding is issued on the PBG Grant Card or by Certified Bank Check — non-payable, with no repayment schedule.

About Prime Business Grant

A verified national gateway for grant applicants.

Prime Business Grant turns every request into a structured, trackable grant application — identity, address, requested tier, processing fee, payout preference, and signature, all in one reviewable file.

It serves citizens, sole proprietors, registered businesses, nonprofits, and community operators. Choose a tier, review the fee, submit, and receive a tracking number instantly.

Approved funding is issued by PBG Grant Card or certified check within 7–14 business days. Nothing is repaid.

PBG Operating Principles
  • Every application is a trackable review file.
  • Citizen and business pathways, side by side.
  • Clear notification after each review stage.

Applicant Process

Four steps from intake to disbursement.

  1. IStep
    Register Applicant Profile

    Submit verified identity, residence, and contact details to open a PBG grant application.

  2. IIStep
    Select Assessment Tier

    Choose one of twelve tiers to align your funding request with the fixed processing-fee schedule.

  3. IIIStep
    Confirm Payout & Notification

    Elect PBG Grant Card or Certified Bank Check payout and select an email or SMS notification route.

  4. IVStep
    Submit & Track Grant Application

    Sign and submit; the portal issues a tier-scoped tracking number for continuous status lookup.
